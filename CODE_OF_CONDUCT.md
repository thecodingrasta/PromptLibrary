# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss the work rather than attacking
the person. Welcome questions, explain disagreement constructively, and respect
other people's time and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual attention, deliberate disruption
and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable. These rules
apply in repository discussions, issues, pull requests and project spaces.

Report a concern privately to a repository maintainer. Do not repeat private
material in a public issue. Maintainers will assess reports, avoid unnecessary
disclosure, and take proportionate action. This can include removing content,
requesting a change in behaviour, restricting participation or banning an account.
A maintainer involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome alone.

Enforcement decisions may be appealed privately to another maintainer where one
is available. No promise of confidentiality overrides an immediate safety need,
but information should only be shared with people who need it to address the case.
