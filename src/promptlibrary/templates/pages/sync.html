Backups and remote snapshots

Snapshots include prompts, versions, projects, workflows, stages, saved runs, categories, tags, labels, statuses and stored preferences. Remote credentials and machine-specific settings are not exported.

This is explicit snapshot transfer, not automatic two-way sync. Merge imports only new or identical records. Conflicting edits stop the entire import. Restore replaces the library after saving a local safety snapshot.

WebDAV

Use HTTPS. Redirects are not followed. The collection must already exist.

SFTP

Unknown or changed host keys are rejected. Verify the server's fingerprint through a trusted channel first. Atomic uploads require the OpenSSH POSIX rename extension.

S3-compatible storage

Changing a destination or username does not reuse its old credentials. Saved credentials are encrypted with a key in the data directory. Protect that directory and the server account.
